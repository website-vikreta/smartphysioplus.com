# Replying to Google reviews

For the clinic owner. This is a guide, not a web page. Replies appear under the clinic's name, so write them the way you would speak to a patient in the room.

## Every reply

- Keep it short and warm. Two to four sentences is enough.
- Thank the patient by first name.
- Name the service they received, so it reads naturally ("thank you for coming in for your knee rehabilitation").
- Reply in plain language. No marketing words, no promises.
- Sign with your name and the clinic name.

## Never

- Confirm a medical detail the patient did not mention themselves. If they wrote "back pain", do not write "your disc bulge". Even confirming that someone is a patient can disclose private information.
- Promise or imply results, cures or timelines.
- Offer a discount, gift or anything else in return for a review, or for changing a review.
- Argue in public, defend yourself point by point, or correct the patient's account.
- Ask the patient to post, change or remove a review.
- Ask staff, family or friends to post reviews.

## Positive reviews

> Thank you, [first name]. We are glad your visit for [service] went well. It was a pleasure to look after you. Dr. Nileema and the team at Smart Physio+

Use the patient's own words for the service. If they did not say which service they had, thank them for visiting instead.

## Negative reviews

1. Wait before replying. Read it twice. Do not reply while upset.
2. Thank them for the feedback and say you are sorry their experience fell short.
3. Do not discuss their care or treatment in public.
4. Offer to talk by phone and give the clinic number from the Contact page.
5. Reply once. If they respond, take it offline.

> Thank you for telling us, [first name]. We are sorry your visit did not go as it should have. We would like to understand what happened. Please call us on [clinic phone] so we can talk it through. Dr. Nileema, Smart Physio+

## Suspected fake or abusive reviews

Do not reply with accusations. Report the review to Google from the Business Profile, and write a short neutral reply: "We cannot find a record of this visit. Please call us so we can look into it." Do not name anyone.

## Asking for reviews

- Ask every patient in the same way. Never ask only the patients who seem happy.
- Use the card (`/review/print/`) or the WhatsApp message on `/review/`. Do not change the wording to suggest what to write.
- Aim for a steady rhythm of about 3 to 5 reviews a week, not a sudden burst.
